Roles in scope for this prompt:
- Staff (Employee)
- Manager / Approver
- Visitor (only in terms of how their choices affect staff/manager)
- System (automatic reminders & auto-cancel timing)

Key scope details to cover:
1) Staff can create visitor requests with:
   - Visitor details (name, phone, email, company)
   - Visit date/time
   - Optional meeting room requirement
   - Optional parking requirement
   - Optional buffet requirement
   - Selected outbound communication channels (QR, WhatsApp, SMS, Email)
2) Some staff require manager approval:
   - Requests go into “Pending Approval”
   - Manager can approve or reject, with optional reason.
3) Some staff bypass manager approval:
   - Their requests can be auto-approved.
4) On approval:
   - System plans reminders & auto-cancel for the visitor:
     - Reminder 1 after 2 working hours.
     - Reminder 2 after another 2 working hours.
     - Auto-cancel 1 hour after second reminder.
   - Must consider working hours (office start and end times).
5) Visitor can accept or reject invitation:
   - Staff sees visitor’s decision.
   - Manager (if any) may also be notified.
