Here is the frontend handoff.

1. Stop expiring QR codes using meeting end alone
Remove logic such as:

isExpired = now >= meetingEnd;
Use the backend’s current values:

Endpoint	Fields
GET /api/v1/visits	Each row’s isExpired, qrExpiresAt
GET /api/v1/visits/:id	isExpired, qrExpiresAt, movementHistory
GET /api/v1/visits/:id/movement-history	qrValidity and data
GET /api/v1/invites/:token	expiresAt, qrCode, canAccept, canReject

Important: isExpired: false does not mean access has already activated. Keep the 60-minute-before-start distinction, and let the backend authorize gate actions.

2. Update the QR status display
Show these states clearly:

Not active yet: before the activation window.
Valid: within the allowed window.
Retained after meeting end: “Valid until checkout or 9 PM.”
Expired: use the backend’s expiry state.
Revocation pending: access is logically expired, but device cleanup is still pending.
For the retained message, use qrValidity.retainedUntilCheckout together with meeting end having passed. Checkout before meeting end still allows re-entry.

Do not present qrExpiresAt: 9 PM as guaranteed validity until 9 PM—checkout after meeting end can expire it earlier.

3. Update movement history
Render each row’s displayLabel, rather than translating every checked_in event into the same label.

Preserve:

Parking arrival
Reception check-in
Reception checkout
Administrative completion—not physical departure
Use qrEffect for supporting text:

Value	Suggested text
arrival_recorded	Arrival recorded
reentry_allowed_before_meeting_end	Re-entry allowed before meeting end
expired_on_checkout	QR expired on checkout
visit_closed_not_physical_departure	Visit closed administratively; not a recorded departure
invalid_schedule_access_denied	Invalid schedule; access denied

Replace updated timeline rows by their stable ID, rather than only appending. Delayed scans can correct a provisional completion time.

4. Preserve Actual IN/OUT meaning
Use:

latestCheckInAt
latestCheckOutAt
These are independent latest reception movements. Do not substitute parking arrival for Actual IN, or administrative completion for Actual OUT.

5. Keep the visitor QR page accessible while retained
Do not hide the QR merely because meeting end passed.

Use the invitation endpoint’s response:

Display the returned QR while valid.
Respect canAccept and canReject.
Handle HTTP 410 Gone as expired/cancelled access.
Do not enable late acceptance just because an existing QR remains valid.
6. Refresh and handle exceptions
Refresh affected list/detail/history data after check-in or checkout.
Refresh on returning to the page.
While an active visit is open, use bounded polling if no live-update mechanism exists.
Show scheduleWarning: "invalid_legacy_schedule" without inventing an expiry date.
Respect existing role permissions and handle 403 responses.
Rollout order: publish the backend through controlled QA, connect these frontend changes, then verify browser flows and physical readers together. The external frontend has not been changed in this workspace.
