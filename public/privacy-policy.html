Privacy Policy
Last Updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

Dallah Albaraka Group ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Visitor Management System (VMS) mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information

  • Full name and contact information (email address, phone number)
  • Profile photograph (optional)
  • Company/organization affiliation
  • Government-issued identification details (for visitor verification)
  • Vehicle information (license plate, make, model) for parking and valet services

Technical Information

  • Device identifiers for push notification delivery
  • App usage data and interaction logs
  • Crash reports and diagnostic data (via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • IP address and general location data

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Manage visitor registrations, approvals, and check-ins
  • Provide parking and valet services
  • Send push notifications about visit status updates and approvals
  • Manage meeting room and buffet bookings
  • Authenticate and authorize users based on their assigned roles
  • Improve application performance and user experience
  • Ensure the security of our facilities and systems

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your data with:

  • Authorized personnel within Dallah Albaraka Group who need access for visitor management purposes
  • Service providers who assist in operating the VMS (e.g., cloud hosting, push notification services)
  • Law enforcement or regulatory authorities when required by applicable law

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including encryption in transit (TLS/SSL), secure authentication with JWT tokens, and role-based access controls. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Visit records may be retained for security audit purposes as required by applicable regulations.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access and review your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of non-essential push notifications
  • Withdraw consent for optional data processing

8. Third-Party Services

Our application uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications
  • Firebase Crashlytics for crash reporting and diagnostics
  • Microsoft Azure Active Directory for single sign-on authentication

Each service has its own privacy policy governing data processing.

9. Children's Privacy

This application is designed for business use and is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy within the application. Your continued use of the VMS after such modifications constitutes your acknowledgment of the modified policy.
