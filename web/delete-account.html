Account & Data Deletion Request
📋
Organization-managed application. The Dallah Albaraka VMS is an internal enterprise application. All user accounts are created and managed by your organization's IT or HR administrators — not through self-registration. Account deletion requests must therefore be submitted through your organization.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request the deletion of your account and associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Contact your organization's IT Administrator or HR Department and submit a written account deletion request.
  2. Include your full name and the email address linked to your VMS account in the request.
  3. Your administrator will verify your identity and submit the deletion request through the VMS administration panel.
  4. You will receive a confirmation once your account and data have been removed. This process typically takes up to 30 days.

What Data is Deleted

The following table describes what happens to each category of your data upon account deletion:

Data Type Action Retention Period
Name, email address, phone number Deleted Immediately upon deletion
Profile photo Deleted Immediately upon deletion
Visit history and records Retained Retained for audit and compliance purposes per organization policy (typically 1 year)
Notification preferences Deleted Immediately upon deletion
Device push notification tokens Deleted Immediately upon deletion
Crash and diagnostic logs Retained Automatically purged within 90 days by Firebase
